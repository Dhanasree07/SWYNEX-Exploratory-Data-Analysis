# SWYNEX-Exploratory-Data-Analysis
Exploratory Data Analysis of a cleaned Netflix dataset using Python.

## Project Overview

This project is part of my internship at **SWYNEX Technologies**.

The objective of Task 2 is to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on the cleaned Netflix dataset prepared during Task 1. The analysis focuses on understanding the dataset, identifying patterns and trends, and extracting meaningful insights using Python.

## Objectives

* Understand the structure of the cleaned dataset
* Analyze the distribution of Netflix content
* Identify trends and patterns
* Explore categorical and numerical variables
* Detect unusual values and anomalies
* Generate meaningful insights from the data

## Tools and Technologies

* Python
* Pandas
* NumPy
* Matplotlib
* Seaborn
* Google Colab
* GitHub

## Dataset

The analysis was performed using the cleaned Netflix dataset from **Task 1**.

The dataset contains information about Netflix titles, including details such as:

* Title
* Content type
* Director
* Cast
* Country
* Date added
* Release year
* Rating
* Duration
* Genres

## Exploratory Data Analysis

The following areas were explored during the analysis:

### 1. Dataset Overview

The dataset structure, number of records, columns, data types, and missing values were examined to understand the overall quality and structure of the data.

### 2. Movies and TV Shows

The distribution of Movies and TV Shows was analyzed to understand the composition of Netflix content in the dataset.

### 3. Content Trends

The number of titles added over different years was analyzed to identify changes and trends in Netflix's content library.

### 4. Country-wise Analysis

The countries associated with Netflix titles were explored to identify countries contributing a significant amount of content.

### 5. Genre Analysis

The most frequently occurring genres were analyzed to understand the types of content commonly available in the dataset.

### 6. Release Year Analysis

The distribution of titles across release years was examined to understand the age and recency of the available content.

### 7. Rating Analysis

Content ratings were analyzed to understand the distribution of titles across different audience categories.

### 8. Movie Duration Analysis

Movie durations were analyzed to understand the typical length of movies and identify unusually short or long titles.

### 9. Anomaly Detection

Statistical analysis was used to identify unusual values and potential outliers in numerical data such as movie duration.

## Key Insights

### Insight 1 — Content Type

The dataset contains both Movies and TV Shows, with differences in their representation within the Netflix catalog.

### Insight 2 — Content Trends

The analysis of titles added over the years reveals changes in Netflix's content additions and helps identify periods with higher or lower numbers of additions.

### Insight 3 — Country Distribution

Netflix content is distributed across multiple countries, with some countries contributing significantly more titles than others.

### Insight 4 — Genre Distribution

The genre analysis shows that certain genres occur more frequently than others, indicating common content categories within the dataset.

### Insight 5 — Release Year

The release-year analysis provides an overview of the distribution of older and newer titles in the Netflix dataset.

### Insight 6 — Movie Duration

Movie duration analysis shows the typical length of movies and helps identify unusually short or long titles that may require further investigation.
